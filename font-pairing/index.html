    The case for slower software
    
          
    Priya Raman · October 2, 2026
    
          
    Every product team I know has a dashboard that measures speed: time to first value, time to close, time saved. Few of them measure the time a person spends understanding what just happened. When software moves faster than the people using it, the savings show up on the dashboard and the confusion shows up in support tickets.
    
          
    The fastest flow is the one people only have to learn once.
    
          
    Slowing down does not mean adding steps. It means choosing the one moment in a flow where a person should pause, and designing that moment with the same care as the happy path.
