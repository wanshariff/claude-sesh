How it works

From blank page to handoff in three steps

  1. Pick fonts

    Generate pairings from 148 hand-classified Google Fonts, add any of Google's 1,800+ families, or bring your Adobe Fonts web project. Lock any role you already like.

  2. Set color

    Enter a brand hex. Kernhue builds an 11-step OKLCH scale, neutrals and status colors, and grades every step for contrast.

  3. Export

    Copy CSS variables, Tailwind v3 or v4, W3C design tokens, or a Markdown spec your team can review.

Proof, not vibes

Every color is graded against WCAG 2.2

Kernhue measures the contrast of every step against white and black text and labels it the same way everywhere in the lab, the preview and the exports.

  • Passes AAA7:1 or more. Safe for body text.
  • Passes AA4.5:1 or more. Fine for UI text and links.
  • AA large only3:1 or more. Headings 24px and up, or icons.
  • Fails AAUnder 3:1. Decorative use only.

Measured live in your browser for #2b63f0. Text color is whichever of white or black scores higher.

FAQ

Questions

Can I use these fonts commercially?

Google Fonts are open source (OFL or Apache) and free for commercial use. Adobe Fonts are licensed through your Adobe subscription and only load from your own web project. Kernhue doesn't host or redistribute any font files.

What happens to my data?

Nothing leaves your browser. Saved pairings, your Adobe project ID and panel settings live in local storage on this device. Share links carry your settings in the URL itself, not on a server.

How do I cancel?

There's nothing to cancel. Kernhue has no accounts or payments today. When paid plans launch, you'll be able to cancel any time from your account and keep your exports.

Do I need an Adobe account?

No. Google Fonts work out of the box. Connect an Adobe Fonts web project only if you want to pair fonts you already license there.

Which contrast standard does Kernhue use?

WCAG 2.2 contrast ratios: 4.5:1 for normal text (AA), 7:1 for enhanced (AAA) and 3:1 for large text and UI graphics.
